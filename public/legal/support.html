OneTap Waiter support

OneTap Waiter is used with a OneTap POS computer in the same venue. It cannot take orders on its own.

Connect to the till

  1. Turn on the POS computer and open OneTap POS.
  2. Join the same Wi-Fi network on this phone or tablet.
  3. Open OneTap Waiter and scan for the till, or type the computer’s local IP address.
  4. Sign in with the staff PIN created on the POS.

iPhone local network

If the app cannot find the till, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn on OneTap Waiter, then try again.

Pairing

Some venues require a pairing code from Admin → Settings on the POS before a new device can sign in.

For further help, contact the person who installed OneTap POS at your venue, or the publisher listed on this app’s store page.
