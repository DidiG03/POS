Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

OneTap Waiter is a companion app for the OneTap POS system used in restaurants and shops. It connects to the venue’s POS computer on the local Wi-Fi network so staff can take orders at the table.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the iOS and Android OneTap Waiter apps published by OneTap. The POS computer software is installed on-site and is not distributed through the App Store or Google Play.

Data we collect

The Waiter app does not run its own cloud account and does not show ads. Order, menu, staff PIN, and payment data stay on the venue’s POS computer. The phone or tablet may keep, on the device only:

  • the address of the POS computer on the local network
  • a session token after a staff member signs in with their PIN
  • display preferences such as language and theme

We do not sell personal data. We do not use the device advertising identifier.

Local network

The app asks for Local Network access so it can find and talk to the POS computer on the same Wi-Fi. Traffic stays on that network. If the venue also uses a printer or fiscal service, that traffic is sent by the POS computer, not by this app.

Children

The app is intended for workplace use by staff aged 16 and over.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support details listed with this app on the App Store or Google Play, or ask the venue manager who operates the POS computer.
