### Payments and fiskalizimi
- **The cash limit now covers the whole bill.** easyPos refuses an invoice over 500,000 ALL that has any cash on it, even €100 of it. The till used to take such a payment, close the table and leave the invoice refused. With fiskalizimi on, a bill over 500,000 ALL (100,000 ALL for a sale to a business with a NIPT, from 30 January 2026) can now only be paid fully by card. Any payment with cash on it is refused before anything is recorded, with "Bills over 500,000 ALL must be paid fully by card in Albania". To take some cash, split the bill by seat into bills each under the limit; each bill is its own invoice. Card-only payments are never limited, and tills not filing in Albania are unaffected. *This rule is based on easyPos's refusal message; it has not been re-tested against the easyPos DEMO service.*
- **Cash received and change.** The till now records what the guest handed over and the change, next to the bill, and prints "Cash received" and "Change" on the receipt. The Pay screen used to send the bill rounded to a whole unit. The sale and the Z report are still based on the bill.
- **The Pay button includes the tip.** The button shows the total charged with the tip, for example "Pay • €44.50 (incl. tip €4.50)". It used to show the bill alone.
- **Older sales without an invoice go on the review list.** Once, after the update, the till looks for sales from before 0.2.114 that never got an invoice and never appeared under Settings › Fiskalizimi › Reviews. That includes invoices easyPos had refused but which were still being re-sent as if easyPos were down. They are put on the review list. Nothing is sent.
- **A void after a table move no longer flags an earlier sale.** Voiding a line on a sitting moved onto a table that had just been paid could put that table's earlier, already filed sale into fiscal reviews. A void now checks only invoices that belong to the bill still open.

### Backups
- **Automatic backups (off unless switched on).** In Admin › Settings › Backups, switch on "Automatic backups". The till then makes a backup after every Z report and every few hours (6 by default). It checks each backup after writing it, copies it to a chosen folder (a USB stick or a network or synced folder) and checks the copy too, and keeps the newest 30 in each place. Backups run in the background, so service never waits. A folder that can't be reached is refused when you save it. A failed or day-old backup shows on the till-health banner, and admins are told.

### Phones and tablets
- **Older Waiter apps can reprint again.** A phone on 0.2.113 or older reprints by the invoice code it sends, or by table, paid time and total. The till still prints its own stored copy of the receipt. If the till can't match the request to a sale, the phone is told "Update the Waiter app", never a silent failure.
- **Till health names phones and tablets on an older app** than the till, with their versions.

### Notes
- "Split equally" still shows each guest's share only and takes one payment. To take shares separately (each its own invoice), pay by seat.
