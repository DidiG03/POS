### Payments and fiskalizimi
- **Albania's cash limit is enforced before payment.** With fiskalizimi on, the till refuses the cash part of a sale above 500,000 ALL, or above 100,000 ALL for a sale to a business with a NIPT (from 30 January 2026), before any payment or invoice is created. Euro bills are converted at the configured Kursi EUR. Card payments are not limited, and for cash + card only the cash part counts. The Pay screen (till, Waiter app and web waiter) warns first and offers **Take card** or **Cash + rest on card**. Tills not filing in Albania are unaffected.
- **Refused invoices go on the review list.** easyPos marks some refusals, the cash limit among them, as a server fault. The till used to keep re-queueing those invoices as if easyPos were down, so they never showed for review. Any refusal easyPos answers with a 4xx now goes on Settings › Fiskalizimi › Reviews, counts in the Z report's "Waiting for review", and shows on the till-health banner. The cash-limit refusal is explained in plain words.
- **Tips.** The Pay screen takes an optional tip, as an amount or 5/10/15%; for cash + card, choose whether the tip is in cash or on the card. The tip is stored on the payment, apart from the sale: it is not in the sale total, the VAT base or the tax invoice. Receipts show the tip and the total paid. The Z report lists tips by method and by waiter, and cash tips count in the cash expected in the drawer.
- **"The till is busy" instead of database errors.** If the ledger stalls during a payment, staff see "The till is busy, so the payment was not taken. Try again in a moment." instead of database error text. After 15 seconds, "Paying…" says the till is slow and that nothing will be charged twice. Retrying with the same payment still records exactly one payment and one invoice.
- **Reprints, replays and approvals.** A reprint prints the till's stored receipt of the sale it names. A payment request whose code was already used is answered only for its own table. A manager's approval of a discount or service-charge removal covers that one payment only, as approved.
- **Refunds of cash + card sales** are split between cash and card in the proportion the sale was paid, on the tax document and in the Z report's cash.

### Waiters
- After orders queued on a bad link are sent, the table's bill is read again at once. It no longer shows 0.00 for up to a minute; until the till has the order, it shows "Updating the bill from the till…".
- The waiter page opened in a browser at the till (`http://<till>:3333/` or `https://<till>:3443/`) now connects to that till without `?backend=`.
- "Cannot reach the till" now appears only after several missed checks in a row with nothing else getting through, not on a single slow answer.
- A missing guest count no longer holds up **Pay**.
- "Split equally" is now "Share per guest (to show only)": it shows each share and takes one payment. To take shares separately, pay by seat.
- With **Print receipt** off, a payment from a phone is recorded without printing, as on the till.

### Other fixes
- Voiding a table with no open bill is refused (`NO_OPEN_BILL`) instead of answering "ok".
- The service charge can be removed in settings (saving it as `null`), not only switched off.
- Fiskalizimi records of sales that are filed and saved are removed after 48 hours, and refused ones after 7 days. Records still waiting for a person are always kept.
- Table moves are recorded on the Voids & discounts screen, which also offers its newer filters.
- Lookbacks for long sittings follow how long the venue lets tables stay open (up to 72 hours).
- "Latest" bills, owners, tickets and print jobs are found by order of entry, so an older row can no longer be taken for the newest.
- Reservation guests' names and phone numbers are anonymised after a set time (12 months by default).
- The Admin app builds without the till's backup code.

### Notes
- Phones and tablets need this version for reprints. Update the waiter phones together with the till.
- Tips are kept out of the tax invoice. Whether a tip must be on the Albanian fiscal invoice has not been confirmed with an accountant. This version has no setting to include tips on the invoice.
