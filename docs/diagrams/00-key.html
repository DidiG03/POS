Glossary

Till / hostThe main OneTap POS computer. Holds the database and runs every business rule; all other apps talk to it.
IPCHow the till's own windows talk to the till program (inside the same computer).
LAN APIHow phones, tablets, Admin and the kitchen display talk to the till over the venue Wi-Fi (HTTP).
Live events (SSE)Messages the till pushes to every device the moment something changes, so screens update without refreshing.
Pairing / device tokenA phone or tablet proves it belongs to the venue with the pairing code from Admin, and keeps a token for it.
Session / tokenProof that someone signed in with their PIN; lasts 12 hours.
Idempotency keyA unique ID created once per tap (Send, Pay). A retry carries the same ID, so the till can tell a repeat from a new action and never does it twice.
SittingOne group of guests at a table, from opening the table to paying.
TicketLogA snapshot of a table's bill, written at every Send. The latest one is the current bill.
Order + PaymentThe saved sale, written once the table is paid. Reports read these.
PrintJobA record of something sent to a printer, and its retries.
KDSKitchen display system: screens in the kitchen that show orders; cooks bump them when done.
Pre-billThe bill printed for the guest before paying (not a fiscal receipt).
CoversNumber of guests at a table.
FiskalizimiReporting every sale to the Albanian tax office as an invoice.
easyPos / CISeasyPos is the service the POS sends invoices to; it files them with the tax office's system (CIS).
NSLF / NIVF / EICCodes on a fiscal invoice: NSLF is generated by the POS side, NIVF is the tax office's registration number, EIC is for electronic invoices.
docId / claimThe fixed ID of one invoice, and a record written before sending it, so a crash or retry can never file it twice.
DeferredAn invoice that could not reach the tax office yet; the sale went through and it is resent automatically.
VaultThe encrypted form of the till's database.
SyncStateA table of named settings and records (venue settings, fiscal claims, layouts…).
Clock-only rolesStaff who only clock in and out on the POS: kitchen, runners, bartender, barback, busser, cleaner, host.
